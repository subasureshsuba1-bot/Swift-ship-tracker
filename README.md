 Stop and Save the Recording

After finishing the presentation:

1. Press Windows + Alt + R again, or click the Stop Recording button in the recording panel.

2. Wait for the recording to finish saving.

3. Open the following folder on your laptop:
   
   Videos → Captures

4. Locate the recorded video and play it once to verify that the screen, audio, and demo output are clear.

If the recording is saved in a different location, check the recording settings or the notification shown after recording stops.
